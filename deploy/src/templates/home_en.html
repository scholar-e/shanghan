How it helps

Not a shortcut to conclusions.
A clearer path to evidence.

Frame questions around the text, then follow connections among clauses, terminology, patterns, and formulas.

01

Find and compare text

Search the Fuling edition by clause number, term, or presentation and inspect corresponding text in context.

Try: “Compare Fuling 10.12 with its corresponding clause.”

02

Trace pattern logic

Examine how signs, mechanism, and treatment principles relate across six-channel differentiation.

Try: “How are aversion to cold and a floating pulse understood in Taiyang?”

03

Read formulas in context

Review composition, dosage, preparation, and related clauses without separating a formula from its textual setting.

Try: “Show the source composition and preparation of Guizhi Tang.”
